About Us

How East Kilbride Damp Specialists works and what to expect when you get in touch.

What We Do

East Kilbride Damp Specialists is a local lead generation and referral service. We are not surveyors, damp proofers, or contractors ourselves. Our role is to connect homeowners who have a damp or mould problem with trusted local specialists who can help.

When you submit an enquiry through this site, we pass your details to a vetted local specialist who will contact you directly to discuss your situation and arrange an assessment if appropriate.

How It Works

1

You Submit an Enquiry

Describe your situation using the contact form — what you have noticed, where it is, and how long it has been present.

2

We Review Your Enquiry

We confirm it is within our service area and relates to a damp or mould issue before passing it on.

3

A Local Specialist Contacts You

A vetted local specialist gets in touch to discuss your situation and arrange a visit if appropriate.

Why Local Specialists

We only work with specialists who operate locally in the East Kilbride area. Local specialists understand the housing stock, can respond more quickly, and are not adding travel costs to their prices. They also tend to be more accountable to their local reputation.

No Obligation

Submitting an enquiry does not commit you to anything. A specialist will be in touch to discuss your situation, and any decision about whether to proceed with a survey or treatment is entirely yours. We do not charge homeowners for this service.
