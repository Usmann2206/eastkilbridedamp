Damp Specialist in East Kilbride

Professional damp surveys and assessments arranged with trusted local specialists.

Damp is one of the most frequently reported property concerns among homeowners in East Kilbride. It can appear gradually, often dismissed as a minor cosmetic issue, before developing into something that affects the structure of your home and the wellbeing of the people living there.

Signs You May Need a Damp Specialist

  • Patches of discolouration on walls or ceilings, particularly after wet weather
  • A persistent musty or earthy smell in certain rooms
  • Peeling or bubbling wallpaper and paint
  • Black or greenish mould appearing on walls, window frames, or in corners
  • Tide mark staining near the base of interior walls
  • Plaster that feels soft, crumbles, or shows white salt deposits
  • Condensation that is consistently worse in one area of the property

What a Damp Survey Involves

1

Visual Inspection

A detailed look at affected areas, noting visible signs and patterns that indicate the likely cause.

2

Moisture Readings

Specialist equipment measures moisture levels within walls and floors to identify the extent of the problem.

3

Cause Identification

The specialist determines whether the issue is rising damp, penetrating damp, condensation, or a combination.

4

Summary and Recommendations

A clear explanation of findings and recommended next steps before any decisions are made.

Damp in East Kilbride Properties

East Kilbride was developed from the 1950s onwards as one of Scotland's new towns. Many properties have experienced gradual deterioration of original damp proof courses, ageing cavity wall construction, and drainage issues. Scotland's high rainfall and cold winters create conditions where damp problems are common. Local specialists understand these patterns and can often identify the likely cause quickly.

If you are a landlord in the East Kilbride area, professional damp assessments are relevant to your obligations under Scottish housing legislation.
