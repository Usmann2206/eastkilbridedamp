Mould Removal in East Kilbride

Professional mould treatment arranged with trusted local specialists.

Mould in the home is more than a visual problem. Depending on the type and extent of growth, it can affect indoor air quality and cause or aggravate respiratory conditions. In East Kilbride homes, mould is often associated with a combination of moisture, limited ventilation, and cold surfaces.

Common Signs of a Mould Problem

  • Black, green, or grey patches on walls, ceilings, or around window frames
  • Mould appearing in corners of rooms, particularly at ceiling level
  • A musty smell that persists even after cleaning and airing
  • Condensation regularly forming on windows and walls
  • Mould returning shortly after surface cleaning
  • Soft or stained plaster in affected areas

If mould is returning after cleaning, this indicates an ongoing moisture source that surface treatment alone will not resolve.

How Mould Treatment Works

1

Assessment

The specialist assesses the extent of growth and identifies the type present to determine the appropriate treatment approach.

2

Moisture Source Identification

The cause of the moisture is identified before treatment begins to ensure the mould does not return.

3

Professional Treatment

Mould is treated using appropriate products and techniques with proper protective equipment where required.

4

Prevention Recommendations

Advice on addressing the root cause through ventilation improvements, structural repairs, or damp proofing.
