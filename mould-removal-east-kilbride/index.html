Mould Removal in East Kilbride

Professional mould treatment arranged with trusted local specialists across East Kilbride.

Mould in the home is more than a visual problem. Depending on the type and extent of growth, it can affect indoor air quality and cause or aggravate respiratory conditions. In East Kilbride homes, mould is often associated with a combination of moisture, limited ventilation, and cold surfaces, though the underlying cause varies from property to property.

Professional treatment addresses both the mould itself and the conditions allowing it to grow.

Common Signs of a Mould Problem

  • Black, green, or grey patches on walls, ceilings, or around window frames
  • Mould appearing in corners of rooms, particularly at ceiling level
  • A musty smell that persists even after cleaning and airing
  • Condensation regularly forming on windows and walls
  • Mould returning shortly after surface cleaning
  • Soft or stained plaster in affected areas

If mould is returning after cleaning, this typically indicates an ongoing moisture source that surface treatment alone will not resolve. A specialist assessment will identify whether the cause is condensation, penetrating damp, or a structural issue.

How Mould Treatment Works

1

Assessment

The specialist assesses the extent of mould growth and identifies the type present to determine the appropriate treatment approach.

2

Moisture Source Identification

The cause of the moisture — whether condensation, penetrating damp, or a structural issue — is identified before treatment begins.

3

Mould is treated using appropriate products and techniques for the surface and severity, with proper protective equipment and disposal where required.

4

Prevention Recommendations

Advice on addressing the root cause so the mould does not return, whether that involves ventilation improvements, structural repairs, or damp proofing.

Mould and East Kilbride Homes

Many properties in East Kilbride experience condensation-related mould, particularly in bathrooms, kitchens, and poorly ventilated bedrooms. Older properties with single-glazed windows or insufficient insulation are particularly susceptible during the colder months Scotland is known for. Some cases involve penetrating damp through ageing external walls or roof structures, which requires a different approach to condensation-related mould.

A local specialist familiar with the area will be able to give you an honest assessment of whether the issue is primarily a ventilation and condensation problem or whether structural damp is involved.

Why Professional Treatment Matters

Over-the-counter mould sprays treat visible mould on the surface but rarely eliminate spores within the material itself. A professional treatment uses appropriate products, protective equipment, and disposal methods, which is particularly relevant where large areas or certain mould types are involved.
