Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

East Kilbride Damp Specialists (“we”, “us”, “our”) operates eastkilbridedamp.co.uk. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

What Data We Collect

When you submit an enquiry through this site, we collect your name, email address, phone number, postcode, and the details you provide about your situation.

How We Use Your Data

We use the information you provide solely to connect you with a local specialist relevant to your enquiry. Your details are passed to one vetted local contractor only.

How Long We Keep Your Data

We retain enquiry records for a period of 12 months for operational and administrative purposes, after which they are securely deleted.

Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. To make a request, contact us via the enquiry form on this site.

Third Parties

We do not sell or share your data with any third party other than the local specialist your enquiry is passed to. We use Web3Forms to process form submissions securely.

Cookies

This site uses standard analytics cookies to understand how visitors use the site. You may disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

Contact

For any privacy-related queries, please use the contact form on this site.
