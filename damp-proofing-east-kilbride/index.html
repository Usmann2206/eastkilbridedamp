Damp Proofing in East Kilbride

Long-term damp proofing solutions arranged with trusted local specialists.

Damp proofing refers to treatments and systems used to prevent moisture from entering or rising through the walls and floors of a property. For many East Kilbride homeowners, damp proofing becomes relevant when an existing damp proof course has failed or penetrating damp is identified following a survey.

When Damp Proofing May Be Needed

  • Visible damp patches on interior walls, particularly at lower levels
  • A failed or absent damp proof course identified during a survey
  • Water penetrating through external walls during or after rainfall
  • Damp problems in a basement or below-ground area
  • Recurring damp issues that have not been resolved by previous treatment
  • Salt deposits or tide marks on internal walls near floor level

Not every damp problem requires damp proofing. A professional assessment will confirm whether it is the appropriate solution for your situation.

Types of Damp Proofing

1

Chemical DPC Injection

A water-repellent chemical is injected into the mortar course to create a new barrier against rising damp. Most common for failed damp proof courses in older properties.

2

Waterproof Render and Tanking

Specialist waterproof coatings applied to walls or floors, most commonly used in basements and areas subject to water pressure from outside.

3

Cavity and Drainage Solutions

Improvements to drainage, cavity walls, or external pointing recommended where penetrating damp is involved.
